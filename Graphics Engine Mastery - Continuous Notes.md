## Phase 6-10 mathematical learning route

Phase 6 establishes vectors, coordinates, and geometry; Phase 7 treats matrices as maps and changes of basis; Phase 8 develops solves, decompositions, and matrix functions; Phase 9 models change with derivatives, integrals, ODEs, and optimization; Phase 10 uses probability, sampling and numerical analysis to reason about trustworthy computation; Phase 11 turns frames and geometry into calibrated images and viewing rays.
