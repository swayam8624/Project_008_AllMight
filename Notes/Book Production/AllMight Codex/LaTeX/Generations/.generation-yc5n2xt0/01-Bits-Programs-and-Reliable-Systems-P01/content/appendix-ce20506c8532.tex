\hypertarget{appendix-ce20506c8532-signed-magnitude}{}

\textbf{Curriculum depth:} Introduced · \textbf{First seen:} C002 · \textbf{Latest development:} C002

\begin{allmightroledefinition}

\subsection{Definition and mechanism}\label{appendix-ce20506c8532-definition-and-mechanism}

\hypertarget{appendix-ce20506c8532-definition-and-mechanism}{}

The high bit selects sign while remaining bits encode magnitude; this is intuitive but has positive and negative zero and needs sign-aware arithmetic.

\end{allmightroledefinition}

The high bit holds sign and the remaining bits hold magnitude. Eight-bit +5 is \BookInlineCode{00000101}, −5 is \BookInlineCode{10000101}, and \BookInlineCode{00000000} and \BookInlineCode{10000000} both denote zero. Mixed signs require magnitude comparison and subtraction rather than ordinary raw-bit addition.
