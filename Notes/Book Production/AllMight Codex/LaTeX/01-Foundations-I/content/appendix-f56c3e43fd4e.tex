\subsection{Definition and mechanism}\label{definition-and-mechanism}

Placement new or \texttt{std::construct\_at} initializes an object in storage already provided by the caller; it does not allocate that backing storage. The storage must have adequate size and alignment. \texttt{std::destroy\_at} ends an object's lifetime but does not release the storage. A pool/container must track occupancy, partial construction, exactly-once destruction, and when reuse is permitted. Transparent replacement and \texttt{std::launder} have detailed preconditions; launder neither constructs nor revives an object.
