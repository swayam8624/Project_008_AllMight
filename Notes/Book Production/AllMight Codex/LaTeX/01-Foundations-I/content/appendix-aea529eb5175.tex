\subsection{Definition and mechanism}\label{definition-and-mechanism}

A span or string\_view describes data without owning it. Copying a view copies its descriptor, not its backing storage. Reference captures and captured this pointers can outlive their referents; copying an owning string into the closure creates a separate owned snapshot.
