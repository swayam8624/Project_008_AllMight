\subsection{Definition and mechanism}\label{definition-and-mechanism}

noexcept is an escaping-exception contract; violation terminates. move\_if\_noexcept selects const T\& when a throwing move and usable copy make rollback safer, otherwise T\&\&. It casts rather than transfers, and non-copyable throwing moves may weaken operation-specific guarantees.
