\section*{Visual laboratory: an accuracy claim needs a convergence curve}
\noindent For \(y'=-y,\;y(0)=1\), the exact solution at \(T=1\)
is \(e^{-1}\). Forward Euler with \(N\) equal steps gives
\(y_N=(1-1/N)^N\). The points below plot the absolute error
\(\lvert y_N-e^{-1}\rvert\) for \(N=2,4,\ldots,256\);
the reference line \(0.184h\) shows a first-order asymptotic guide.
