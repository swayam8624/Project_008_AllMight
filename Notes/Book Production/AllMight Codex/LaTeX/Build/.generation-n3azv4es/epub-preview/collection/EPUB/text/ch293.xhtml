The short route

Convention card: active right-handed rotations; column vectors; y = M x ; rightmost operation first; code angles in radians; Hamilton quaternion components ( w , x , y , z ) . Row-major storage does not choose any of those mathematical conventions.

Four experiments to reconstruct without the chapter

  1. Send ( 4 , 2 ) through columns ( 2 , 1 ) and ( − 1 , 3 ) . Obtain ( 6 , 10 ) by column contributions and row sums. Explain why changing storage cannot change that answer.
  2. Compare observations ( 2 , 2.001 ) and ( 2 , 2.002 ) for A = [ 1 1 1 1.001 ] . Recover ( 1 , 1 ) versus ( 0 , 2 ) and identify the source of uncertainty.
  3. For q Z = ( 2 / 2 , 0 , 0 , 2 / 2 ) , rotate X to Y. Then use 2 q Z : true inverse still gives Y, conjugate sandwich gives four times Y, and the unit-optimized formula is outside its contract.
  4. Compare the columns of S R 45 ∘ with S = diag ( 2 , 1 ) . Their dot is − 3 / 2 , proving that the result is not an orthogonal rotation times diagonal scale.
