Reconstruction checkpoint — project a Gaussian before optimizing the rasterizer

tools/labs/gaussian_projection_reference_lab.cpp now serves as the independent CPU oracle for the most important screen-space transformation. It finite-differences the pinhole camera projection and compares the result with the analytic Jacobian; propagates a positive-semidefinite 3D covariance through J Σ J T ; checks projected symmetry and nonnegative eigenvalues; verifies the expected quadratic scaling with focal length; exercises low-order spherical-harmonic directionality; and demonstrates that front-to-back alpha composition has order-independent final opacity but generally order-dependent color.

That is deliberately narrower than “Gaussian renderer complete.” Conic inversion, tile duplication/prefix scan, stable tile-depth sorting, higher-order SH, native GPU rasterization, temporal low-pass behavior and current Maveb/Vulkax parity remain their own milestones. Gaussian covariance projection and footprint, Gaussian alpha compositing and order, and Captured-world similarity alignment carry the derivations and the Sim(3) covariance scale rule into the editing/reconstruction story.
