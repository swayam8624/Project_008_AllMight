Multiscale texture filtering

Curriculum depth: Introduced · First seen: XR01 · Latest development: XR01

Definition and mechanism

Mipmaps prefilter texture scales; anisotropy reflects unequal footprint directions. Gaussian pyramids store coarse structure; Laplacian levels store differences between scales. A texel is not an output pixel, and interpolation cannot ensure lost information is recovered.

Prerequisites

  • Convolution and filter footprints (Geometry, Cameras, and Image Formation, Part 1, Reference notes: Convolution and filter footprints) — A weighting footprint establishes the filtered response, boundary effects and frequency transfer.
  • Sampling aliasing and reconstruction (Light Transport and Image Synthesis, Part 1, Reference notes: Sampling aliasing and reconstruction) — Allocation/filtering must respect what reduced samples can distinguish.
