Barycentric coordinates and interpolation

Curriculum depth: Developed · First seen: Phase6 · Latest development: University courses

Definition and mechanism

Barycentric weights reconstruct a triangle point as an affine combination. The weights sum to one; nonnegative weights describe points inside or on a nondegenerate triangle. Dot-system solves on off-plane inputs describe their projection, not proven surface membership.

The panel sensor ( 1 , 1 , 0 ) has weights ( 5 / 12 , 1 / 4 , 1 / 3 ) and corner temperatures ( 20 , 40 , 50 ) interpolate to 35 .

University course development

Extend the existing interpolation note with the geometric meanings that make triangle rasterization and camera calibration easier to reason about. For triangle vertices p 0 , p 1 , p 2 , a point inside can be written p = λ 0 p 0 + λ 1 p 1 + λ 2 p 2 , where the weights sum to one and are nonnegative. Each weight is the area of the opposite subtriangle divided by the full triangle area; equivalently it is a signed edge-distance normalized by altitude. The weights therefore provide both a point-in-triangle test and attribute interpolation. They also reveal a limitation: screen-space weights do not linearly interpolate a 3D attribute after perspective projection. Interpolate reciprocal depth and attribute divided by depth, then divide the interpolated values to recover perspective-correct texture coordinates or colors.
