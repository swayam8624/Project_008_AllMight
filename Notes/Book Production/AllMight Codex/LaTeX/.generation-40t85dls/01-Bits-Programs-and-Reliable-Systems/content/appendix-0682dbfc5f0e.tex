\section{Packed structures and misaligned access}\label{appendix-0682dbfc5f0e-packed-structures-and-misaligned-access}

\hypertarget{appendix-0682dbfc5f0e-packed-structures-and-misaligned-access}{}

\textbf{Curriculum depth:} Introduced · \textbf{First seen:} C003 · \textbf{Latest development:} C003

\begin{allmightroledefinition}

\subsection{Definition and mechanism}\label{appendix-0682dbfc5f0e-definition-and-mechanism}

\hypertarget{appendix-0682dbfc5f0e-definition-and-mechanism}{}

Compiler packing extensions can weaken aggregate/member alignment and remove holes. A uint32 member at offset one may be misaligned. Direct extension-aware member access may receive special code generation; extracting an ordinary typed pointer can lose that treatment. Hardware tolerance does not establish legal C++ access. Explicit codecs avoid alignment, lifetime, and host-layout assumptions.

\end{allmightroledefinition}
