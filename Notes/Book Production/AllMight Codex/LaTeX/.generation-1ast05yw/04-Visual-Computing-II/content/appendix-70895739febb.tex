\subsection{Definition and mechanism}\label{appendix-70895739febb-definition-and-mechanism}

Motion turns spatial variation into temporal variation: a scalar moving grating has frequency \textbar v\textbar f.~Retinal velocity depends on scene and eye motion in a common frame. Frame Nyquist limits and human flicker thresholds are different questions.
