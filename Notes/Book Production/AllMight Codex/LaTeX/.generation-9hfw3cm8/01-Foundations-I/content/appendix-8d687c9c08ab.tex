\subsection{Definition and mechanism}\label{appendix-8d687c9c08ab-definition-and-mechanism}

\texttt{C} reports unsigned carry, \texttt{V} signed-range failure, \texttt{N} the result's sign bit, and \texttt{Z} an all-zero architectural result.

For eight-bit addition, C tests exact sum\textgreater255; V tests signed sum outside {[}−128,127{]}; N is result bit 7; Z tests low result==0. \texttt{7F+01} gives 0,1,1,0; \texttt{FF+01} gives 1,0,0,1 in C,V,N,Z order. These are binary arithmetic flags, not decimal-mode behavior.
