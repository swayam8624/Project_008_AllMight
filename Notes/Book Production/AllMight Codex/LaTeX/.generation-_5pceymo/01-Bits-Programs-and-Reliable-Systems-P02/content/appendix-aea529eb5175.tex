\section{Deferred captures and borrowed views}\label{appendix-aea529eb5175-deferred-captures-and-borrowed-views}

\textbf{Curriculum depth:} Introduced · \textbf{First seen:} C008 · \textbf{Latest development:} C008

\begin{allmightroledefinition}

\subsection{Definition and mechanism}\label{appendix-aea529eb5175-definition-and-mechanism}

\hypertarget{appendix-aea529eb5175-definition-and-mechanism}{}

A span or string\_view describes data without owning it. Copying a view copies its descriptor, not its backing storage. Reference captures and captured this pointers can outlive their referents; copying an owning string into the closure creates a separate owned snapshot.

\end{allmightroledefinition}
