# References and bibliography {#bibliography}

This consolidated reading list gathers foundational works, specifications and further reading for the topics developed in this volume. Chapter-specific references and source-custody notes remain in the reader. The list is not a claim that every external source has been exhaustively reviewed or reproduced. The companion implementations are independently authored teaching references; their tested scope is recorded in the repository.

## Geometry, surfaces and numerical robustness

- Shewchuk, Jonathan Richard. “Adaptive Precision Floating-Point Arithmetic and Fast Robust Geometric Predicates.” *Discrete & Computational Geometry*, 18, 305–363 (1997). [Author's resources](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~quake/robust.html). Chapters 03–05.
- Ericson, Christer. *Real-Time Collision Detection*. Morgan Kaufmann (2005). Chapters 05–07.
- Schneider, Philip J., and David H. Eberly. *Geometric Tools for Computer Graphics*. Morgan Kaufmann (2002). Chapters 02–10.
- Möller, Tomas, and Ben Trumbore. “Fast, Minimum Storage Ray–Triangle Intersection.” *Journal of Graphics Tools*, 2(1), 21–28 (1997). Chapter 06.
- Farin, Gerald. *Curves and Surfaces for CAGD: A Practical Guide*, fifth edition. Morgan Kaufmann (2002). Chapter 08.
- Patrikalakis, Nicholas M., Takashi Maekawa, and Wonjoon Cho. *Shape Interrogation for Computer Aided Design and Manufacturing*. [Springer](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-04074-0). Chapters 08–09.
- Pomax. [A Primer on Bézier Curves](https://pomax.github.io/bezierinfo/). Chapter 08.
- Crane, Keenan. [Discrete Differential Geometry course](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~kmcrane/Projects/DDG/). Chapters 07–08 and 30.
- Lorensen, William E., and Harvey E. Cline. “Marching Cubes: A High Resolution 3D Surface Construction Algorithm.” *SIGGRAPH* (1987). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/37402.37422). Chapters 09 and 30.

## Asset formats and computer graphics

- Khronos Group. [glTF 2.0 specification](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html) and [glTF tutorials](https://github.khronos.org/glTF-Tutorials/). Chapters 10–11.
- Library of Congress. Wavefront OBJ file format description. Chapter 10.
- Netpbm. [PPM format specification](https://netpbm.sourceforge.net/doc/ppm.html). Chapters 01 and 21.
- University of California, Berkeley. [CS 184: Computer Graphics](https://cs184.eecs.berkeley.edu/). Further reading for Chapters 01–21.
- Scratchapixel. [Computer graphics lessons](https://www.scratchapixel.com/). Further reading for Chapters 02–23.

## Cameras, optics and color

- Hartley, Richard, and Andrew Zisserman. *Multiple View Geometry in Computer Vision*, second edition. Cambridge University Press (2004). Chapters 13–15, 23 and 26–29.
- W3C. [CSS Color Module Level 4](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/). Chapter 25: sRGB and Display P3 conventions.
- International Commission on Illumination (CIE). CIE 1931 standard-observer colorimetry. Chapter 25: the toy model is not a standard-observer data table.

## Vision, reconstruction and evidence

- Hartley, Richard I. “In Defense of the Eight-Point Algorithm.” *IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence*, 19(6) (1997). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/34.601246). Chapter 27.
- Harris, Chris, and Mike Stephens. “A Combined Corner and Edge Detector.” *Alvey Vision Conference* (1988). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5244/C.2.23). Chapter 28.
- Lowe, David G. “Distinctive Image Features from Scale-Invariant Keypoints.” *International Journal of Computer Vision*, 60 (2004). Chapter 28.
- Rublee, Ethan, Vincent Rabaud, Kurt Konolige, and Gary Bradski. “ORB: An Efficient Alternative to SIFT or SURF.” *ICCV* (2011). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV.2011.6126544). Chapter 28.
- Fischler, Martin A., and Robert C. Bolles. “Random Sample Consensus: A Paradigm for Model Fitting with Applications to Image Analysis and Automated Cartography.” *Communications of the ACM*, 24(6) (1981). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/358669.358692). Chapter 28.
- Schönberger, Johannes L., and Jan-Michael Frahm. “Structure-from-Motion Revisited.” *CVPR* (2016). Chapter 29.
- Triggs, Bill, Philip McLauchlan, Richard Hartley, and Andrew Fitzgibbon. “Bundle Adjustment — A Modern Synthesis.” *Vision Algorithms: Theory and Practice*, LNCS 1883 (2000). Chapter 29.
- Curless, Brian, and Marc Levoy. “A Volumetric Method for Building Complex Models from Range Images.” *SIGGRAPH* (1996). Chapter 30.
