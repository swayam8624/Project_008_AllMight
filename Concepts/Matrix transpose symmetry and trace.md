# Matrix transpose symmetry and trace

**Curriculum depth:** Introduced · **First seen:** Phase7 · **Latest development:** Phase7

## Definition and mechanism

Transpose exchanges row and column indices and reverses product order. Symmetric and skew-symmetric parts reconstruct any real square matrix. Trace is the diagonal sum and is invariant under similarity, while cyclic product trace needs compatible dimensions.

**Reconstruction cue:** (AB)^T=B^T A^T; real skew-symmetric matrices have zero diagonal.
