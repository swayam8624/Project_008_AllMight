# Contrast sensitivity adaptation and masking

**Curriculum depth:** Introduced · **First seen:** XR01 · **Latest development:** XR01

## Definition and mechanism

Contrast uses a stated background/extrema convention. CSF characterizes detection sensitivity; MTF characterizes optical transfer. Adaptation, masking and crowding change visibility/recognition in different ways; peripheral acuity alone does not exhaust perception.
