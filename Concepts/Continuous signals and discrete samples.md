# Continuous signals and discrete samples

**Curriculum depth:** Introduced · **First seen:** XR01 · **Latest development:** XR01

## Definition and mechanism

A signal represents variation over a defined domain. Sampling discretizes locations; quantization discretizes values. Precision cannot recover observations absent from the domain. A gaze trace and an image share mathematics but differ in units and causal requirements.
