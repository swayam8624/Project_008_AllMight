# Spatiotemporal sampling and retinal motion

**Curriculum depth:** Introduced · **First seen:** XR01 · **Latest development:** XR01

## Definition and mechanism

Motion turns spatial variation into temporal variation: a scalar moving grating has frequency |v|f. Retinal velocity depends on scene and eye motion in a common frame. Frame Nyquist limits and human flicker thresholds are different questions.
