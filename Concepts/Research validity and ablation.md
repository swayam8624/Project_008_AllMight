# Research validity and ablation

**Curriculum depth:** Introduced · **First seen:** XR01 · **Latest development:** XR01

## Definition and mechanism

Ablation isolates a method component; reference choice, confounds and uncertainty limit interpretation. External validity concerns conditions beyond the experiment. Software replication and perceptual replication need different evidence, while historical surveys cannot certify current novelty.

## Prerequisites

- [[Psychophysics and threshold inference]] — A perceptual conclusion requires a defined task and inference procedure.
- [[Perceptual metrics and display calibration]] — Ablation conclusions depend on a declared, justified outcome measure.
