# Exterior products and geometric algebra

**Curriculum depth:** Introduced · **First seen:** Phase6 · **Latest development:** Phase6

## Definition and mechanism

Exterior products represent oriented higher-dimensional elements without forcing them into vectors. A wedge is antisymmetric and bilinear; a bivector has grade two. Euclidean geometric algebra combines inner and exterior products into one product. Higher-dimensional sums of bivectors need not be a single plane.

The plane element $I=\mathbf e_1\mathbf e_2$ satisfies $I^2=-1$, connecting plane rotations to complex-number behavior.
