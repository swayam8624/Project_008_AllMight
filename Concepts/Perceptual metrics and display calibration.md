# Perceptual metrics and display calibration

**Curriculum depth:** Introduced · **First seen:** XR01 · **Latest development:** XR01

## Definition and mechanism

MSE/PSNR describe numerical differences under a declared domain; SSIM adds structural comparison. Gaze weighting is an engineering proxy, not validated human truth. Perceptual models require display/viewing/version settings and evidence for intended artifact/task conditions.

## Prerequisites

- [[Contrast sensitivity adaptation and masking]] — Visibility judgments require sensitivity and stimulus-context definitions.
- [[Fourier analysis and spectral phase]] — Frequency and phase reveal transfer, sampling ambiguity and structural loss.
- [[Color encoding and linear-light operations]] — Quality comparisons depend on the interpreted light/encoded domain.
