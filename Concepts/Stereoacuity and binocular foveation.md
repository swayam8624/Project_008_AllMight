# Stereoacuity and binocular foveation

**Curriculum depth:** Introduced · **First seen:** XR01 · **Latest development:** XR01

## Definition and mechanism

Stereoacuity measures a disparity task, not every component of visual quality. Peripheral blur and eye-asymmetric dominance findings have stimulus/observer limits. Per-eye monocular metrics alone do not prove stable binocular perception.

## Prerequisites

- [[Optical focus and binocular conflict]] — Optical image formation determines the direction/focus assumptions used by geometry and binocular analysis.
- [[Psychophysics and threshold inference]] — A perceptual conclusion requires a defined task and inference procedure.
- [[Visual acuity and eccentricity]] — Peripheral allocation uses a specified task-dependent angular sensitivity model.
