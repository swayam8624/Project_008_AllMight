# Quaternion algebra and Hamilton product

**Curriculum depth:** Introduced · **First seen:** Phase7 · **Latest development:** Phase7

## Definition and mechanism

A quaternion has scalar w and vector u. Hamilton multiplication combines scalar product w_a w_b minus dot(u_a,u_b), and vector w_a u_b plus w_b u_a plus cross(u_a,u_b). It is associative and generally noncommutative; component order is a separate interface convention.

**Reconstruction cue:** i times j equals k, while j times i equals minus k.
