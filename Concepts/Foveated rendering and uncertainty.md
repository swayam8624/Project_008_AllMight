# Foveated rendering and uncertainty

**Curriculum depth:** Introduced · **First seen:** XR01 · **Latest development:** XR01

## Definition and mechanism

Foveation allocates work by a declared visual/gaze model; FFR, DFR and VRS change different mechanisms. Display-time uncertainty and gaze-landing tails affect safe allocation. Post-filtering a full reference is a proxy, not saved original rendering work.

## Prerequisites

- [[Visual acuity and eccentricity]] — Peripheral allocation uses a specified task-dependent angular sensitivity model.
- [[Temporal reprojection and history validation]] — Changing quality regions inherit history correspondence and reliability obligations.
- [[Eye movements and display-time gaze]] — Time-aligned gaze/motion defines the retinal and allocation context.
