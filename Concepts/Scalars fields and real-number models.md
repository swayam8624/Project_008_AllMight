# Scalars fields and real-number models

**Curriculum depth:** Introduced · **First seen:** Phase6 · **Latest development:** Phase6

## Definition and mechanism

A scalar is an element of the field used by a vector space. A field supports addition, multiplication and nonzero division under specified algebraic laws. The reals also supply order and completeness. Floating-point values approximate this model but do not form a mathematical field.

An integer reciprocal need not be an integer; $2^{-1}=1/2$. A real scalar can carry metres or seconds. A finite representation cannot preserve every real-number operation.
