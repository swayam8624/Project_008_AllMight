# PSF OTF and MTF

**Curriculum depth:** Introduced · **First seen:** XR01 · **Latest development:** XR01

## Definition and mechanism

PSF is a point's image response; OTF is its Fourier transform; normalized MTF is its magnitude. Gaussian transfer suppresses high-frequency contrast under a linear shift-invariant model. Real optics can vary by position, wavelength and focus.

## Prerequisites

- [[Optical focus and binocular conflict]] — Optical image formation determines the direction/focus assumptions used by geometry and binocular analysis.
- [[Fourier analysis and spectral phase]] — Frequency and phase reveal transfer, sampling ambiguity and structural loss.
- [[Convolution and filter footprints]] — A weighting footprint establishes the filtered response, boundary effects and frequency transfer.
