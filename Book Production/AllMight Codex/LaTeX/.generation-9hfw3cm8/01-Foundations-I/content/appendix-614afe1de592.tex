\section{Rounding to nearest ties to even}\label{appendix-614afe1de592-rounding-to-nearest-ties-to-even}

\textbf{Curriculum depth:} Introduced · \textbf{First seen:} C004 · \textbf{Latest development:} C004

\subsection{Definition and mechanism}\label{appendix-614afe1de592-definition-and-mechanism}

Choose the nearest representable value; exactly halfway choose the even retained significand integer. At toy precision three bits, 1.125→1 and 1.375→1.5. std::round uses halfway-away-from-zero instead.
