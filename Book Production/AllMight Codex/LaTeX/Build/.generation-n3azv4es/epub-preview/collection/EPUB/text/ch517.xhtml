A posteriori error bounds for contractive iteration

Curriculum depth: Developed · First seen: Phase 10 · Latest development: Phase 10

Definition and mechanism

For a fixed-point iteration x n + 1 = G ( x n ) , suppose G is a contraction with Lipschitz constant q < 1 on a region containing the iterates and its fixed point. Then the remaining error is bounded by the next step divided by 1 − q : ‖ x ∗ − x n ‖ ≤ ‖ x n + 1 − x n ‖ / ( 1 − q ) . The assumption is essential; a small step alone does not prove convergence because stagnation or a failed update can also make changes tiny. In implementation, report the contraction evidence or a suitable residual bound and keep failure distinct from success. This turns an observed update into a conditional, checkable estimate rather than a guess. If q = 1 / 2 and the latest step is 10 − 6 , this bound is 2 × 10 − 6 , conditional on the contraction proof.
