Gaussian elimination and pivoting

Equivalent elementary row operations expose pivots and triangular structure while preserving the solution set when applied to the full augmented system. Pivoting avoids zero or dangerously small divisors; it improves the calculation but does not cure an ill-conditioned problem.

Use it when: understanding REF/RREF, LU, consistency, rank, and triangular solves.

Developed in Phase 8: Elimination turns those geometric questions into auditable row operations on the augmented matrix. Pivots expose rank and free variables; partial pivoting chooses a safer divisor and tracks row exchanges, while residual and growth checks explain why pivoting cannot repair ill-conditioning. elimination steps (Mathematics for Visual Computing, Part 1, Ch. 3) · pivoting and growth (Mathematics for Visual Computing, Part 1, Ch. 3)
