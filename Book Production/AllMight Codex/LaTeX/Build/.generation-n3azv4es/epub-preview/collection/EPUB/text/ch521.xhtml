Cubic Hermite interpolation

Curriculum depth: Developed · First seen: Phase 10 · Latest development: Phase 10

Definition and mechanism

A cubic Hermite segment is determined by endpoint values and endpoint tangent data. With normalized t ∈ [ 0 , 1 ] , its four basis polynomials blend the two endpoint positions and two tangents. Tangents are derivatives with respect to the parameter, so physical-time derivatives must be scaled by segment duration: a velocity in units per second is not interchangeable with a tangent per normalized parameter. Hermite curves are useful when a camera path or animation must pass through known positions while exposing direct control over its entry and exit directions. The endpoint constraints determine one cubic polynomial; continuity between adjacent segments still requires compatible endpoint tangents.
