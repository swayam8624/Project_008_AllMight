Conditioning and singularity

Curriculum depth: Developed · First seen: C004 · Latest development: Phase7

Definition and mechanism

Conditioning measures sensitivity to input perturbation. A = 10 − 4 I 2 has determinant 10 − 8 but condition number one in the 2-norm. Uniform scaling changes determinant magnitude without changing this conditioning measure.

Phase 7 develops κ ( A ) = ‖ A ‖ ‖ A − 1 ‖ , near-parallel-column sensitivity, pivoted elimination and inverse residuals. Equal determinants can conceal very different stretch ratios: diag ( 10 − 10 , 10 − 10 ) and diag ( 1 , 10 − 20 ) both have determinant 10 − 20 but conditions one and 10 20 . Full worked sensitivity example (Mathematics for Visual Computing, Part 1, Ch. 2).

Subtraction of nearby inputs exposes input uncertainty relative to a small difference. Sensitivity belongs to the problem; exact subtraction of stored operands cannot recover information lost when those inputs were rounded.
