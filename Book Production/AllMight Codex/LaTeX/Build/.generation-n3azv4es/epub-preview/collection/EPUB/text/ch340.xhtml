Quadric error mesh simplification

Curriculum depth: Developed · First seen: University courses · Latest development: University courses

Definition and mechanism

Quadric error simplification greedily collapses the edge with the least predicted geometric error. For each triangle plane with unit normal n and offset d , form v = ( n x , n y , n z , d ) T and K = v v T . A homogeneous point U = ( x , y , z , 1 ) T has squared point-to-plane error U T K U . Sum the matrices of incident planes at each vertex; an edge candidate combines endpoint matrices. Partitioning the combined quadratic gives Q ( x ) = x T A x + 2 b T x + c , whose stationary point solves A x = − b . If the system is singular or indefinite, test endpoints and midpoint and keep the least-cost candidate. After selecting a location, reject collapses that flip incident face normals. The metric is a local approximation, so topology preconditions and orientation checks remain necessary.
