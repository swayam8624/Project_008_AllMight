Gram matrices and coordinate metrics

Curriculum depth: Introduced · First seen: Phase6 · Latest development: Phase6

Definition and mechanism

A Gram matrix stores pairwise basis inner products. Coordinate vectors measure physical dot products through c T G d . It is positive semidefinite, and positive definite for independent generators. It records metric geometry but not orientation.

The basis ( 1 , 0 ) , ( 1 , 1 ) has G = ( 1 1 1 2 ) . Coordinates ( 1 , 2 ) give squared length 13 , not 5 .
