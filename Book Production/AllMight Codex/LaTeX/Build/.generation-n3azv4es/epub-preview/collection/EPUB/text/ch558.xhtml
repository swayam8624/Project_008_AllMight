Spatiotemporal sampling and retinal motion

Curriculum depth: Introduced · First seen: XR01 · Latest development: XR01

Definition and mechanism

Motion turns spatial variation into temporal variation: a scalar moving grating has frequency |v|f. Retinal velocity depends on scene and eye motion in a common frame. Frame Nyquist limits and human flicker thresholds are different questions.

Prerequisites

  • Sampling aliasing and reconstruction (Light Transport and Image Synthesis, Part 1, Reference notes: Sampling aliasing and reconstruction) — Allocation/filtering must respect what reduced samples can distinguish.
  • Eye movements and display-time gaze — Time-aligned gaze/motion defines the retinal and allocation context.

Used by

  • Temporal reprojection and history validation
  • Optical flow and time to contact (Light Transport and Image Synthesis, Part 1, Reference notes: Optical flow and time to contact)
