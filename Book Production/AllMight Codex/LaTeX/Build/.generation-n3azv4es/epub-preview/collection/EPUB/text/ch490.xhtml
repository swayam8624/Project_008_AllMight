Polar decomposition

The right polar form A = Q H separates an orthogonal factor from a positive-semidefinite stretch H = ( A T A ) 1 / 2 . The orthogonal factor may include reflection; singular matrices can make its completion nonunique. Symmetric stretch can include shear in the chosen coordinates.

Use it when: separating local rigid-like motion from symmetric deformation.

Developed in Phase 8: Polar decomposition factors a map into an orthogonal part and a positive-semidefinite stretch. The SVD constructs both left and right forms, separates reflection from a proper rotation, and gives the nearest orthogonal or special-orthogonal factor when the singular directions allow a unique choice; zero singular values leave completion freedom. left and right stretch (Mathematics for Visual Computing, Part 1, Ch. 8) · nearest proper rotation and ambiguity (Mathematics for Visual Computing, Part 1, Ch. 8)
