Moore-Penrose pseudoinverse

The exact SVD defines A + by reciprocating every positive singular value and transposing the orthogonal factors. A + b minimizes residual first and Euclidean solution norm among minimizers second. A numerical cutoff instead produces A τ + : if it discards a positive singular value, it solves a retained-rank approximation and can increase the residual of the original system. The cutoff makes the treatment of weak directions explicit.

Use it when: rectangular or rank-deficient least squares needs a minimum-norm answer.

Developed in Phase 8: The exact pseudoinverse reciprocates every positive singular value, yielding the least-squares solution with minimum Euclidean norm.
