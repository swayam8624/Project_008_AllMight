Covectors linear functionals and normal transforms

Curriculum depth: Developed · First seen: Phase6 · Latest development: Phase7

Definition and mechanism

Phase 7 derives ( R S ) − T = R S − 1 for orthogonal R and nonzero diagonal scales S , explains why zero scale invalidates inverse-based normal transport, and distinguishes shading-normal direction from determinant-signed cross-derived area. TRS normal derivation (Mathematics for Visual Computing, Part 1, Ch. 3).

A covector is a linear scalar-valued measurement rule. Surface normals represent rules annihilating tangent movement. Under an invertible active linear transform, preserved measurements require inverse-transpose normal transformation. A metric identifies vector and covector representations.

For tangent ( 1 , 1 , 0 ) and normal ( 1 , − 1 , 0 ) , doubling horizontal scale requires normal ( 1 / 2 , − 1 , 0 ) to preserve perpendicularity.
