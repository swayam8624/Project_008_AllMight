\section*{Polar decomposition}
\section{Polar decomposition}\label{appendix-73f5bcf7a462-polar-decomposition}

The right polar form \(A=QH\) separates an orthogonal factor from a positive-semidefinite stretch \(H=(A^\mathsf T A)^{1/2}\). The orthogonal factor may include reflection; singular matrices can make its completion nonunique. Symmetric stretch can include shear in the chosen coordinates.

\textbf{Use it when:} separating local rigid-like motion from symmetric deformation.

\textbf{Developed in Phase 8:} Polar decomposition factors a map into an orthogonal part and a positive-semidefinite stretch. The SVD constructs both left and right forms, separates reflection from a proper rotation, and gives the nearest orthogonal or special-orthogonal factor when the singular directions allow a unique choice; zero singular values leave completion freedom. \hyperref[02-foundations-ii-ch024-polar-decomposition-separates-frame-dependent-stretch-from-orientation]{left and right stretch} · \hyperref[02-foundations-ii-ch024-two-sided-rotations-nearest-proper-rotation-and-nonunique-directions]{nearest proper rotation and ambiguity}
