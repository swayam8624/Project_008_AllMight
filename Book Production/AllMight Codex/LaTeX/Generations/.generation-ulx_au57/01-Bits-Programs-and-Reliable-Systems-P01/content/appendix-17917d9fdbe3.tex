\section{Ripple-carry addition}\label{appendix-17917d9fdbe3-ripple-carry-addition}

\hypertarget{appendix-17917d9fdbe3-ripple-carry-addition}{}

\textbf{Curriculum depth:} Introduced · \textbf{First seen:} C002 · \textbf{Latest development:} C002

\begin{allmightroledefinition}

\subsection{Definition and mechanism}\label{appendix-17917d9fdbe3-definition-and-mechanism}

\hypertarget{appendix-17917d9fdbe3-definition-and-mechanism}{}

Each full adder feeds its carry to the next position, making worst-case circuit propagation depth grow with word width.

\end{allmightroledefinition}

Connect carry-out at bit i to carry-in at i+1. For \BookInlineCode{{0}{1}{1}{1}{1}{1}{1}{1}{\ }{+}{\ }{1}}{01111111\ +\ 1}, a carry traverses the low seven stages. Circuit delay grows with width in this simple design; fixed-width software addition still counts as O(1) with respect to input size.
