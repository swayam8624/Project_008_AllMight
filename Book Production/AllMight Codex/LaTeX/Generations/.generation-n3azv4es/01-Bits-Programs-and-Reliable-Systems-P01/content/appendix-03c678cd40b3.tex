\section{One's complement}\label{appendix-03c678cd40b3-ones-complement}

\hypertarget{appendix-03c678cd40b3-ones-complement}{}

\textbf{Curriculum depth:} Introduced · \textbf{First seen:} C002 · \textbf{Latest development:} C002

\begin{allmightroledefinition}

\subsection{Definition and mechanism}\label{appendix-03c678cd40b3-definition-and-mechanism}

\hypertarget{appendix-03c678cd40b3-definition-and-mechanism}{}

Negation inverts every bit, leaving two zeros and requiring end-around carry for ordinary addition.

\end{allmightroledefinition}

Negative x is the N-bit complement of positive x. Eight-bit −5 is \BookInlineCode{{1}{1}{1}{1}{1}{0}{1}{0}}{11111010}; zeros are all zeros and all ones. Adding 5 and encoded −3 gives a carry plus low value 1; end-around carry adds that carry back to yield 2.
