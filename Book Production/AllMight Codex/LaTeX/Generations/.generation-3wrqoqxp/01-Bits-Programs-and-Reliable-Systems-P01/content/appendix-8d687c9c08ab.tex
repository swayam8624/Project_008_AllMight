\section{Carry, overflow, negative, and zero flags}\label{appendix-8d687c9c08ab-carry-overflow-negative-and-zero-flags}

\hypertarget{appendix-8d687c9c08ab-carry-overflow-negative-and-zero-flags}{}

\textbf{Curriculum depth:} Introduced · \textbf{First seen:} C002 · \textbf{Latest development:} C002

\begin{allmightroledefinition}

\subsection{Definition and mechanism}\label{appendix-8d687c9c08ab-definition-and-mechanism}

\hypertarget{appendix-8d687c9c08ab-definition-and-mechanism}{}

\BookInlineCode{C} reports unsigned carry, \BookInlineCode{V} signed-range failure, \BookInlineCode{N} the result's sign bit, and \BookInlineCode{Z} an all-zero architectural result.

\end{allmightroledefinition}

For eight-bit addition, C tests exact sum\textgreater255; V tests signed sum outside {[}−128,127{]}; N is result bit 7; Z tests low result==0. \BookInlineCode{7F+01} gives 0,1,1,0; \BookInlineCode{FF+01} gives 1,0,0,1 in C,V,N,Z order. These are binary arithmetic flags, not decimal-mode behavior.
