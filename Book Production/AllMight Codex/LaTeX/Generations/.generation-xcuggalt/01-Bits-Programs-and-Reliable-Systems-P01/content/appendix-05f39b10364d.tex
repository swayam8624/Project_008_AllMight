\section{Minimum signed value}\label{appendix-05f39b10364d-minimum-signed-value}

\hypertarget{appendix-05f39b10364d-minimum-signed-value}{}

\textbf{Curriculum depth:} Introduced · \textbf{First seen:} C002 · \textbf{Latest development:} C002

\begin{allmightroledefinition}

\subsection{Definition and mechanism}\label{appendix-05f39b10364d-definition-and-mechanism}

\hypertarget{appendix-05f39b10364d-definition-and-mechanism}{}

Two's-complement range has one extra negative value, so \BookInlineCode{{I}{N}{T}{\_}{M}{I}{N}}{INT\_MIN} cannot be negated or represented by same-type absolute value.

\end{allmightroledefinition}

N-bit two's complement spans −2ᴺ⁻¹ through 2ᴺ⁻¹−1. Eight-bit minimum is −128, whose positive counterpart 128 does not fit. Negating int minimum or dividing it by −1 overflows the operation type. Narrow int8\_t operands may promote first, so the original storage width alone does not establish UB.
