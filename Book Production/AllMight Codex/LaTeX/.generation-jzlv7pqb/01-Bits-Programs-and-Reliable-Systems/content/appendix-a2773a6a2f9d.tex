\subsection{Definition and mechanism}\label{appendix-a2773a6a2f9d-definition-and-mechanism}

Nearly equal magnitudes subtract to a small difference, amplifying prior operand errors by roughly (\textbar a\textbar+\textbar b\textbar)/\textbar a−b\textbar. Stored subtraction can itself be exact. The source binary pair 1753/1024 and 1751/1024 differs by exactly 2\^{}-9.
