\section*{Visual laboratory: a differential equation becomes a field}
\noindent On \((x,y)\in[0,1]^2\), the heat equation with unit diffusivity,
zero Dirichlet boundary values, and initial mode
\(u(x,y,0)=\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y)\) has exact solution
\[
u(x,y,t)=e^{-2\pi^2t}\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y).
\]
These plots evaluate the exact solution at \(t=0.04\).
A finite-difference or mesh solver should converge toward this
reference under a stated resolution and time-integration policy.
