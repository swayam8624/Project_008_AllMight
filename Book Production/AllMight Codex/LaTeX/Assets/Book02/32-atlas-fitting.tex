\section*{Outlier-resistant fitting begins with the objective}
\noindent Thirty-five synthetic observations follow an exponential model with one deliberately added gross outlier. The plot compares ordinary least squares, the independently implemented Huber/LM-like method, a SciPy Huber reference and the original generating curve.

\begin{figure}[!htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{book02-figures/rendered/figures/robust-fitting.pdf}
\caption{The same 35 observations are used for every comparison. The blue markers are synthetic samples; the outlier is explicitly marked. Residual summaries retain all observations rather than silently removing difficult samples.}
\label{fig:book02-atlas-robust-fitting}
\end{figure}

\noindent\textbf{Reconstruction experiment.} Sweep initialization and Huber threshold, compare Jacobians against finite differences, and record the failures as well as the successful solutions.
