\section*{Matrix factorization choices}
\chapter{Matrix factorization choices}\label{appendix-221a483bec2d-matrix-factorization-choices}

Choose a solver from the matrix promises and task: pivoted LU for general square systems, Cholesky for SPD systems, QR for full-rank least squares, SVD or rank-revealing methods when weak directions matter, and iterative methods for suitable large sparse workloads. Reuse factors for repeated right-hand sides.

\textbf{Use it when:} choosing an algorithm before implementing or calling a numerical routine.

\textbf{Developed in Phase 8:} The factorization should follow the matrix structure and the requested result: LU handles general square systems, Cholesky exploits positive definiteness, QR fits full-rank least squares, and SVD handles uncertain rank and spectral questions.
