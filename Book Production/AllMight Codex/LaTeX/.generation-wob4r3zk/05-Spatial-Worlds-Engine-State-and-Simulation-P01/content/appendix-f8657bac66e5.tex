\section{Matrix norms and numerical error}\label{appendix-f8657bac66e5-matrix-norms-and-numerical-error}

\hypertarget{appendix-f8657bac66e5-matrix-norms-and-numerical-error}{}

The spectral norm is the largest 2-norm stretch and equals the largest singular value. The Frobenius norm measures aggregate squared entry energy. Residuals, orthogonality errors, and condition numbers need a declared norm and scale; a small residual alone need not imply a sensitive solution is accurate.

\textbf{Use it when:} setting scaled tolerances or interpreting numerical diagnostics.

\textbf{Developed in Phase 8:} The spectral norm measures worst-case amplification, while the Frobenius norm accumulates squared energy across entries. The chapter uses them to scale residuals, reconstruction errors, and orthogonality defects, and separates backward error in a nearby problem from forward error in the requested answer. \href{../02-Mathematics-for-Visual-Computing-P01/main.pdf\#01-foundations-i-ch019-matrix-norms-put-scale-into-error-statements}{norms and scale (Mathematics for Visual Computing, Part 1, Ch. 3)} · \href{../02-Mathematics-for-Visual-Computing-P01/main.pdf\#01-foundations-i-ch020-residuals-distinguish-backward-accuracy-from-forward-certainty}{residuals and error interpretation (Mathematics for Visual Computing, Part 1, Ch. 4)}

\subsection{A matrix norm can protect a geometry bound}\label{appendix-f8657bac66e5-a-matrix-norm-can-protect-a-geometry-bound}

\hypertarget{appendix-f8657bac66e5-a-matrix-norm-can-protect-a-geometry-bound}{}

A local sphere under a linear transform is generally an ellipsoid. The Frobenius norm times its original radius gives a safe, possibly loose enclosing radius. Taking only the longest matrix column can fail under shear. \hyperref[04-visual-computing-ii-ch028-a-line-corridor-can-be-a-cheap-prefilter-for-shadow-caster-work]{The culling example} derives the bound, demonstrates the shear counterexample, and links its geometric figure. This connects numerical matrix norms to conservative rejection decisions.
